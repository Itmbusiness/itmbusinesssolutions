# Legal content review

The privacy and terms content dated 8 October 2026 is maintained in the public content pack and rendered from the editable `pages` table. Refresh only these two pages with `php artisan db:seed --class=LegalPagesSeeder`. This intentionally replaces legal body/title/SEO copy, preserves existing publication flags and does not reseed other CMS content or settings.

The copy reflects the implemented enquiry, account and staff follow-up features. Future payments, shared organisation workspaces and document-vault features are not described as available. Contact links use the settings-driven contact page so dashboard edits do not leave a stale legal contact address.

Before external publication, the owner and a Zimbabwe-qualified legal adviser should confirm the contracting legal entity and trading-name relationship, relevant data-controller licensing/DPO obligations, actual service providers and processing locations, documented retention periods, operational security measures, sensitive-data handling and the cancellation/refund process. The text does not invent a registration number, licence, designated DPO, exact retention duration or guaranteed encryption/storage location. A legal page is not proof that operational requirements have been met.

Sources checked for drafting, not copied as contract templates:
- POTRAZ-hosted Data Protection Act: https://www.potraz.gov.zw/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/Data-Protection-Act-5-of-2021.pdf
- Government-hosted Consumer Protection Act: https://www.mic.gov.zw/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Consumer-Protection-Act-2019.pdf
- Consolidated legislation and linked subsidiary instruments: https://zimlii.org/akn/zw/act/2021/5/eng%402022-03-11

Confirm current amendments and regulator guidance during professional review, especially cross-border processing. Client-facing wording preserves statutory rights and avoids asserting that consent to an enquiry is consent to marketing or acceptance of a paid quotation.
