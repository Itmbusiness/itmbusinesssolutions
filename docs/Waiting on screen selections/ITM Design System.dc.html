Prompt 01 · Master visual system

ITM Business Solutions

Component reference board. Every later screen — public website, consultant workspace and client portal — inherits the tokens, components and responsive rules defined here.

Assumption to confirm

The attached Nocturne design system is a dark, blurple interface — incompatible with your gold-on-navy mark and the prompt book's off-white page rule. I've kept ITM's palette as the source of truth and inherited Nocturne's method: Inter, tonal ramps in OKLCH, a compact spacing scale, outlined secondary actions, themed focus rings, Phosphor icons.

Naming

Renamed throughout from ITM Business Solutions to ITM Business Solutions, tagline "Ideas today. A brighter tomorrow." Positioning copy still reads corporate-secretarial — say the word if you want it broadened.

Placeholders, deliberately

No statutory fees, authority logos, seals, processing guarantees or client data are invented anywhere. Every fee is a placeholder row; every document preview is neutral.
