Company Registration

Private Limited Company Registration

Register a private limited company and receive the incorporation documents banks, landlords and tender portals ask for.

Reviewed 14 Aug 2026 · Next review Feb 2027
1:1 service thumbnail
never a mock certificate

Is this service right for you?

Choose this if
You want limited liability and a separate legal entity
A bank, landlord or tender portal has asked for a certificate of incorporation
You plan to bring in shareholders or investors
Consider instead
PBC registration — simpler, for a small owner-run business
Trust registration — for family or asset-holding purposes
Association or PVO — for non-profit activity

Common questions

How long does registration take?
Processing times are set by the authority and change. Your consultant gives a current estimate at assessment — ITM does not guarantee a turnaround.
Why do I get a quotation before paying?
Because your situation changes the work and the statutory fees. A written quotation lets you see professional fees, authority charges and optional costs separately before committing.
Can I register a company on my own?
Yes. Clients usually use ITM to avoid rejections on names, share structures and incomplete documents, and to have one contact following the file.
