# ITM Corporate Secretarial Website PRD and Content Pack

This pack defines a technology-neutral sales, service-delivery and compliance-support platform for ITM Corporate Secretarial.

## Confirmed product decisions

- The specification is technology-neutral.
- Clients request a quotation, approve it and then pay.
- One client account can manage several organisations.
- Organisation owners can invite team members and assign permissions.
- Public visitors can enquire or request a quotation without first creating an account.
- Each of the 20 services has an individual SEO landing page.
- Articles are governed by reviewed-on and next-review dates.

## Folder guide

- `PRD.md` — master product requirements document.
- `specifications/` — information architecture, roles, flows, data, SEO, security and analytics.
- `content/public/` — public marketing and utility pages.
- `content/categories/` — seven SEO category landing pages.
- `content/services/` — twenty complete service-page content specifications.
- `content/articles/` — article hub, template, editorial plan and starter drafts.
- `content/auth/` — authentication and invitation pages.
- `content/consultant-dashboard/` — consultant and back-office pages.
- `content/client-dashboard/` — client organisation-workspace pages.
- `page-inventory.csv` — route-to-file inventory for implementation and QA.

## Content placeholders

The following must be supplied before production launch:

- Confirmed logo and brand guidelines
- Physical address and verified contact information
- WhatsApp and telephone numbers
- Accepted payment methods and bank or merchant details
- Professional fee schedule
- Current statutory fees
- Actual turnaround ranges supported by operations
- Privacy contact and formal legal entity details
- Verified testimonials and case studies

## Compliance warning

Service requirements, fees, forms, portals and deadlines can change. Pages marked as regulated content require review by an authorised consultant before publication and on the review cadence defined in `specifications/10-content-governance.md`.
