- Organisations formalising governance

- Initial structure and purpose questionnaire

## Frequently asked questions

### Is this legal advice?

Website information is general. Matters requiring legal interpretation should be reviewed by an appropriately qualified professional.

### Who can be a trustee?

Suitability, number and eligibility should be assessed under current law and the intended structure.

### Can a trust own property?

Asset ownership and transfer implications require case-specific legal and tax assessment.

### Is every trust registered in the same way?

No. Purpose, structure and applicable process can differ, which is why assessment comes before quotation.
