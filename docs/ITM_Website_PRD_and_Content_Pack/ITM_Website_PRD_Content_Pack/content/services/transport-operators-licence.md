- Operation and licence-type assessment

## Frequently asked questions

### Is one licence valid for every vehicle?

Vehicle, route and operating-authority rules depend on the licence type and current transport requirements.

### Are roadworthiness and insurance documents required?

Relevant vehicle and operational compliance evidence may be required and should be confirmed during assessment.

### Can approval be guaranteed?

No. The responsible transport authority makes licensing decisions.

### Can you manage renewals for a fleet?

Yes. Each vehicle and licence can be stored with its own status, conditions and renewal reminders.
