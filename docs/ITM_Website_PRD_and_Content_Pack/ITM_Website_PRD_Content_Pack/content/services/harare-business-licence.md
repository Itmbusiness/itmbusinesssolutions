## Frequently asked questions

### Does every business use the same licence?

No. Requirements may depend on activity, premises, location and sector.

### Is an inspection required?

Some activities or premises may require inspections or additional clearances. This must be confirmed for the specific application.

### Can you guarantee the licence?

No. Approval depends on the municipality and applicable inspections or requirements.

### Can you handle renewals?

Yes, after checking the current licence, expiry date and any changed business information.
