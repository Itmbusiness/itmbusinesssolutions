## Frequently asked questions

### Is a TIN the same as a tax-clearance certificate?

No. A taxpayer identifier identifies the taxpayer, while a tax-clearance certificate relates to compliance status for a defined period.

### Can you obtain tax clearance if returns are outstanding?

Outstanding obligations normally need assessment and resolution before a clearance request can succeed.

### How long is tax clearance valid?

Check the issue and expiry dates on your current certificate. We can help you review its status against current official guidance.

### Do you guarantee issuance?

No. Issuance depends on the taxpayer record, submitted information and the responsible authority.
