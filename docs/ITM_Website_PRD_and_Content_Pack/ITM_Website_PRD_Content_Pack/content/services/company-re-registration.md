- Existing-records assessment

## Starting requirements

- [ ] Existing registration certificate and company number
- [ ] Available company forms and constitutional documents

## Frequently asked questions

### Is company re-registration still open?

Please ask us to check the current re-registration window and the requirements for your existing records before you pay. Deadlines and consequences can change through official notices.

### What if some old documents are missing?

The consultant will first identify the missing records and advise whether replacement or reconstruction steps are required.

### Does re-registration change ownership?

It should not be presented as an ownership-change service. Any intended changes must be disclosed and quoted separately.

### Can I pay before my records are reviewed?

The recommended process is assessment first, followed by a written quotation based on the actual work required.
