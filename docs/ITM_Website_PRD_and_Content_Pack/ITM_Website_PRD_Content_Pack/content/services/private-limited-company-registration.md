## Frequently asked questions

### Can one person own the company?

Ownership and director requirements depend on the applicable rules and the proposed structure. The consultant will assess your intended setup before quotation.

### Can I register while outside Zimbabwe?

Remote assistance may be possible if the required identity, address and authority information can be supplied. Any local-presence requirements will be explained during assessment.

### Is tax registration included?

Only when it is listed in the approved quotation or selected package. Company registration and tax registration are shown as separate line items.

### Is approval guaranteed?

No. Final acceptance, names and processing times remain subject to the responsible authority.
