## Frequently asked questions

### Does every group need registration?

The appropriate level of formalisation depends on activities, funding, contracts and stakeholder requirements.

### Can you draft the constitution?

Document drafting can be included subject to scope and any required specialist review.

### Who owns the association?

Associations are membership-based structures; governance and control should be defined in the constitution.

### Can an association open a bank account?

Bank requirements vary. The final document pack should be checked against the chosen bank’s current onboarding rules.
