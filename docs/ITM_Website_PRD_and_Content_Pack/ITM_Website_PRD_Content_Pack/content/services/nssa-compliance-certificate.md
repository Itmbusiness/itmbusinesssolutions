Identify outstanding employer actions before a compliance-certificate request is submitted.

## Is this service right for you?

- Employers preparing for tenders
- Suppliers undergoing due diligence
- Employers renewing compliance documents
- Businesses correcting contribution or return gaps

If the correct route depends on your circumstances, ITM will assess the information before asking you to approve a quotation.

## What the service can include

- Employer-compliance assessment
- Outstanding-action list
- Quotation for agreed corrective work
- Certificate-request support when eligible
- Expiry date captured for reminders

The final deliverables are the items written in the approved quotation. Authority decisions, third-party processing and items outside the quotation are excluded.

## Starting requirements

- [ ] Employer registration details
- [ ] Available contribution and return records
- [ ] Employee information for affected periods
- [ ] Prior certificate or notices
- [ ] Payment evidence where relevant
- [ ] Portal access through an approved secure method

This is a preparation checklist. A consultant may request fewer or additional items after reviewing the organisation and current official process.

## How it works

1. Complete a compliance assessment.
2. Review identified return or payment gaps.
3. Approve the corrective-work quotation.
4. Resolve agreed outstanding actions.
5. Request the certificate when eligibility conditions are met.
6. Store the result and schedule the next reminder.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can a certificate be issued with outstanding obligations?

Eligibility depends on the employer record. Outstanding returns, contributions or discrepancies may need to be resolved first.

### Can a certificate be verified?

The NSSA self-service portal provides certificate-verification functionality.

### Do you guarantee issuance?

No. The authority determines compliance status and issuance.

### Will the dashboard remind us before expiry?

Yes, once the issue and expiry dates are recorded for the organisation.
