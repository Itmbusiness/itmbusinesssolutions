## Frequently asked questions

### When should an employer register?

NSSA’s published guidance states that employers must register within 30 days of becoming employers. Confirm the current rule when acting.

### What information is needed?

The official self-service flow requests company, contact, banking, director and contact-person information, with additional fields depending on the employer.

### Does registration include monthly returns?

No, unless recurring-return support is included in the quotation or package.

### Can employees be added later?

Employee records and ongoing changes should be managed through the applicable employer process after registration.
