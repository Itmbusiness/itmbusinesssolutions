## Frequently asked questions

### Is a PVO the same as a trust?

No. Structures, governance and regulatory responsibilities differ. Suitability should be assessed before proceeding.

### How long does registration take?

Processing can depend on readiness, current procedure and authority review. Use a dated estimate rather than a guarantee.

### Can you guarantee approval?

No. The responsible authority makes the decision.

### Can we operate before registration?

Operational and fundraising implications require current legal guidance specific to the organisation.
