## Frequently asked questions

### Is there one national vendor number?

Vendor onboarding may be specific to the buying organisation or system. The exact target must be identified before quotation.

### Is PRAZ registration the same as a vendor number?

No. They can be related in procurement, but they are separate processes and requirements.

### Can you complete banking forms for us?

The client must verify and approve all banking details before submission.

### Do you guarantee onboarding?

No. The buying organisation controls approval and may request further evidence.
