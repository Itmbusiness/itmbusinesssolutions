- Businesses supplying the public sector
- Contractors
- Professional service providers
- Existing suppliers renewing or updating categories

If the correct route depends on your circumstances, ITM will assess the information before asking you to approve a quotation.

## What the service can include

- Supplier-readiness assessment
- Organisation profile checklist
- Category-selection support within scope
- Application or renewal assistance
- Status and client-action updates

The final deliverables are the items written in the approved quotation. Authority decisions, third-party processing and items outside the quotation are excluded.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does registration guarantee a tender?

No. Registration supports eligibility or onboarding but does not guarantee selection, award or payment.

### What is a supplier coordinator?

The eGP account uses an identified contact to manage the organisation profile. The client should retain access to the official email and credentials.

### Are authority fees included?

Only if shown as a separate line in the approved quotation.

### Can you help with renewals?

Yes. Renewal dates and category information can be stored in the organisation compliance calendar.
