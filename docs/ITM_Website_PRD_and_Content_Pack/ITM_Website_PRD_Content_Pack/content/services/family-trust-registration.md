## Frequently asked questions

### Does a family trust protect every asset?

No blanket promise should be made. Outcomes depend on lawful structure, asset transfers, timing and professional advice.

### Will ownership transfer automatically?

No. Each asset may require a separate lawful transfer and may have tax, consent or registration implications.

### Can one family member control everything?

Trustee powers and governance must be designed carefully and reviewed for the intended purpose.

### How often should the trust be reviewed?

Review after major family, asset, legal or tax changes and at a regular interval agreed with advisers.
