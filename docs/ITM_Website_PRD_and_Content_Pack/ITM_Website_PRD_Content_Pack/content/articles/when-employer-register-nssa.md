# When Must an Employer Register with NSSA?

The right answer depends on the organisation’s activities, records and intended outcome. This guide explains the preparation work and questions that should be resolved before a quotation or application begins.

## Registration timing matters

NSSA’s published registration guidance states that employers must register within 30 days of becoming employers. Current guidance should still be checked when acting.

## Prepare organisation information

The official workflow requests company, contact, banking, director and contact-person information.

## Plan beyond registration

Employer setup leads to recurring contribution, employee and return responsibilities. These should be added to the organisation calendar.

## Keep authorised contacts current

Official account recovery depends on accessible contact information. Update records when responsible people change.


## Common mistakes to avoid

- Paying before the exact service and exclusions are written down.
- Relying on an undated checklist or social-media post as current authority guidance.
- Giving a consultant permanent control of the organisation’s official email or portal account.
- Assuming registration guarantees a certificate, tender, licence or legal outcome.
- Mixing records belonging to different organisations.

## What to do next

Prepare the available records and request an assessment. ITM will identify missing information and send a written quotation before payment is requested.
