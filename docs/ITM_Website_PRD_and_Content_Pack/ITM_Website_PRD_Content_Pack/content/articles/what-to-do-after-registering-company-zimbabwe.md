# What to Do After Registering a Company in Zimbabwe

The right answer depends on the organisation’s activities, records and intended outcome. This guide explains the preparation work and questions that should be resolved before a quotation or application begins.

## Check the issued records

Confirm names, identifiers, addresses, directors and ownership information before using the records elsewhere.

## Create a compliance map

Assess tax, employer, municipal, sector and procurement obligations based on actual activities—not a generic checklist alone.

## Set up controlled record keeping

Store final registration records, approvals, contracts and deadlines in one organisation workspace with appropriate team permissions.

## Prepare how the business will present itself

A consistent logo, company profile, contact channel and invoicing process help the new entity operate professionally.


## Common mistakes to avoid

- Paying before the exact service and exclusions are written down.
- Relying on an undated checklist or social-media post as current authority guidance.
- Giving a consultant permanent control of the organisation’s official email or portal account.
- Assuming registration guarantees a certificate, tender, licence or legal outcome.
- Mixing records belonging to different organisations.
