# Trust, PVO or Association: Which Structure Fits the Purpose?

The right answer depends on the organisation’s activities, records and intended outcome. This guide explains the preparation work and questions that should be resolved before a quotation or application begins.

## Begin with purpose and activity

Charitable programmes, membership activities, family stewardship and asset holding raise different governance and regulatory questions.

## Who makes decisions?

Identify founders, trustees, office bearers, members and beneficiaries, then define accountability and succession.

## How will money or property be handled?

Funding, assets, contracts and reporting needs should be assessed before documents are drafted.

## Use qualified review

The choice can have legal and tax consequences. Website content should guide preparation, not replace case-specific professional advice.


## Common mistakes to avoid

- Paying before the exact service and exclusions are written down.
- Relying on an undated checklist or social-media post as current authority guidance.
- Giving a consultant permanent control of the organisation’s official email or portal account.
- Assuming registration guarantees a certificate, tender, licence or legal outcome.
- Mixing records belonging to different organisations.
