# PBC vs Private Limited Company in Zimbabwe

The right answer depends on the organisation’s activities, records and intended outcome. This guide explains the preparation work and questions that should be resolved before a quotation or application begins.

## Start with the business you intend to run

Choosing a structure should begin with ownership, management, customers, contracts and expected growth—not only the cheapest registration price.

## Where the structures differ

Explain ownership terminology, governance, administrative expectations and perceived suitability using current reviewed information. Avoid claiming that one structure is automatically better for every founder.

## Questions to answer before choosing

How many owners are involved? Will the business seek large contracts? Will ownership change? Does a bank, investor or customer expect a specific structure?

## Why assessment matters

Registry rules and transition procedures can change. A current eligibility and requirements check should come before quotation and payment.


## Common mistakes to avoid

- Paying before the exact service and exclusions are written down.
- Relying on an undated checklist or social-media post as current authority guidance.
- Giving a consultant permanent control of the organisation’s official email or portal account.
- Assuming registration guarantees a certificate, tender, licence or legal outcome.
- Mixing records belonging to different organisations.
