# PAYE, VAT, QPD and Income Tax: A Practical Introduction

The right answer depends on the organisation’s activities, records and intended outcome. This guide explains the preparation work and questions that should be resolved before a quotation or application begins.

## Different obligations serve different purposes

Tax types should be assessed individually. Registering a company does not prove that every tax type applies in the same way.

## Records drive accurate returns

Payroll, sales, expenses and prior submissions need organised source records. A submission workflow cannot repair missing commercial records by itself.

## Approval before submission

An authorised client should review figures, assumptions and periods before a consultant submits a return.

## Build a recurring calendar

Once obligations are confirmed, save frequencies, due dates, document requests and responsible team members in the organisation workspace.


## Common mistakes to avoid

- Paying before the exact service and exclusions are written down.
- Relying on an undated checklist or social-media post as current authority guidance.
- Giving a consultant permanent control of the organisation’s official email or portal account.
- Assuming registration guarantees a certificate, tender, licence or legal outcome.
- Mixing records belonging to different organisations.

## What to do next

Prepare the available records and request an assessment. ITM will identify missing information and send a written quotation before payment is requested.
