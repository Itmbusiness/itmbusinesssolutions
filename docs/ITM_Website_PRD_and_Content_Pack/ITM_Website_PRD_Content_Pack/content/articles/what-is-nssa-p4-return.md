# What Is an NSSA P4 Return?

The right answer depends on the organisation’s activities, records and intended outcome. This guide explains the preparation work and questions that should be resolved before a quotation or application begins.

## Purpose of the return

NSSA explains that the P4 provides a breakdown of employee contributions and supports crediting individual member accounts.

## Monthly workflow

The published guidance describes the P4 as a monthly return accessed and e-filed through the employer self-service portal.

## Information should be reviewed

Employee, earnings and contribution information must be checked by an authorised person before filing.

## Make recurring work visible

Use scheduled document requests, approval checkpoints, submission evidence and the next-period reminder.


## Common mistakes to avoid

- Paying before the exact service and exclusions are written down.
- Relying on an undated checklist or social-media post as current authority guidance.
- Giving a consultant permanent control of the organisation’s official email or portal account.
- Assuming registration guarantees a certificate, tender, licence or legal outcome.
- Mixing records belonging to different organisations.
