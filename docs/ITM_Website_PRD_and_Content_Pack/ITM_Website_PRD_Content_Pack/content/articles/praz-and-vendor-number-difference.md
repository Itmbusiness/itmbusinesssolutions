# PRAZ Registration and Vendor Numbers: What Is the Difference?

The right answer depends on the organisation’s activities, records and intended outcome. This guide explains the preparation work and questions that should be resolved before a quotation or application begins.

## PRAZ supplier registration

PRAZ’s eGP process maintains a supplier organisation profile and category information for public procurement participation.

## Buyer-specific vendor onboarding

A vendor number may belong to a particular institution or buying system. Requirements can therefore differ between buyers.

## Documents often overlap

Company, tax, banking, director and contact records may be requested in both contexts, but one approval should not be presented as automatically replacing the other.

## Registration is not a tender award

Supplier onboarding helps an organisation participate or transact; it does not guarantee selection or a contract.


## Common mistakes to avoid

- Paying before the exact service and exclusions are written down.
- Relying on an undated checklist or social-media post as current authority guidance.
- Giving a consultant permanent control of the organisation’s official email or portal account.
- Assuming registration guarantees a certificate, tender, licence or legal outcome.
- Mixing records belonging to different organisations.
