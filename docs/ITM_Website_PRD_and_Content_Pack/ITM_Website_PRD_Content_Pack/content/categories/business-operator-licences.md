# Business and Operator Licences

Prepare for municipal or sector-specific licensing with a clear checklist, document review and application support.

**CTA:** Request a Category Assessment

## Who this category helps

- Retailers
- Office-based businesses
- Transport operators
- Businesses opening new premises

## Services

### [Harare Shop and Business Licence Support](/services/harare-business-licence)

Prepare for a Harare shop or business licence application with premises, activity and document requirements assessed first.

### [Transport Operator’s Licence Support](/services/transport-operators-licence)

Prepare a transport-operator licensing application with the vehicle, operator and route requirements assessed first.


## How the quotation process works

1. Select the service or describe the outcome.
2. Provide initial organisation information.
3. A consultant identifies missing requirements and confirms scope.
4. Receive a written quotation separating professional and statutory charges.
5. Approve the quotation before payment.
