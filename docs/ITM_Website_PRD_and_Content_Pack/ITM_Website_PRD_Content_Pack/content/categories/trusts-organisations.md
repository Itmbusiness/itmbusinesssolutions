# Trusts and Organisations

Receive guided formation support for trusts, family structures, associations and private voluntary organisations.

**CTA:** Request a Category Assessment

## Who this category helps

- Families planning asset stewardship
- Community groups
- Charitable initiatives
- Membership organisations

## Services

### [Trust Registration](/services/trust-registration)

Receive guided trust-formation support with an initial purpose, trustee and document-requirements assessment.

### [PVO Registration](/services/pvo-registration)

Prepare a private voluntary organisation registration project with governance, programme and document support.

### [Association Registration](/services/association-registration)

Formalise a membership or community association with a clear purpose, constitution and leadership structure.

### [Family Trust Registration](/services/family-trust-registration)

Plan a family trust with guided collection of purpose, trustee, beneficiary, governance and asset information.


## How the quotation process works

1. Select the service or describe the outcome.
2. Provide initial organisation information.
3. A consultant identifies missing requirements and confirms scope.
4. Receive a written quotation separating professional and statutory charges.
5. Approve the quotation before payment.
