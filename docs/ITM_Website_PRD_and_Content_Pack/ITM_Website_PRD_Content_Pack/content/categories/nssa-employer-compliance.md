# NSSA and Employer Compliance

Register as an employer, organise contribution records and maintain the recurring returns needed for a compliant employer profile.

**CTA:** Request a Category Assessment

## Who this category helps

- New employers
- SMEs with staff
- Growing organisations
- Employers correcting compliance gaps

## Services

### [NSSA Employer Registration](/services/nssa-employer-registration)

Register an organisation as an employer with guided preparation of business, banking, director and contact information.

### [NSSA Compliance Certificate Support](/services/nssa-compliance-certificate)

Assess employer records and receive support toward obtaining or renewing an NSSA compliance certificate.

### [NSSA P4 Returns](/services/nssa-p4-returns)

Organise monthly employee contribution data and receive support preparing and filing NSSA P4 returns.


## How the quotation process works

1. Select the service or describe the outcome.
2. Provide initial organisation information.
3. A consultant identifies missing requirements and confirms scope.
4. Receive a written quotation separating professional and statutory charges.
5. Approve the quotation before payment.
