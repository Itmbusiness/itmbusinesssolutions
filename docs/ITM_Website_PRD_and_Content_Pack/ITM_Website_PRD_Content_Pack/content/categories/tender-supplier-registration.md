# Tender and Supplier Registration

Prepare the organisation information and supporting documents commonly needed for supplier onboarding and procurement opportunities.

**CTA:** Request a Category Assessment

## Who this category helps

- Suppliers
- Contractors
- Service providers
- Businesses targeting institutional buyers

## Services

### [PRAZ Registration Support](/services/praz-registration)

Prepare your organisation profile and supporting documents for supplier registration through Zimbabwe’s eGP process.

### [Vendor Number Support](/services/vendor-number)

Prepare supplier information for vendor onboarding with a target organisation, institution or procurement system.


## How the quotation process works

1. Select the service or describe the outcome.
2. Provide initial organisation information.
3. A consultant identifies missing requirements and confirms scope.
4. Receive a written quotation separating professional and statutory charges.
5. Approve the quotation before payment.
