# Privacy Policy

Production legal review is required before publication.

The final policy must explain the responsible legal entity, information collected, purposes, lawful basis or consent, service providers, cross-border processing where applicable, retention, security, user rights, complaint process, cookies, analytics, document handling and privacy contact.

It must distinguish public enquiry information from authenticated organisation records and explain how invited team members may access organisation information according to their roles.
