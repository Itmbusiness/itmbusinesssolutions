# Frequently Asked Questions

## Why do I receive a quotation before paying?

Requirements and the amount of work can depend on the organisation’s current position. Assessment prevents payment for an unsuitable or incomplete service.

## Are government fees included?

Your quotation separates professional fees, statutory charges and optional expenses.

## Do you guarantee approval?

No. ITM prepares and supports applications, while the responsible authority controls acceptance and processing.

## Can one account manage several companies?

Yes. One account can belong to several organisation workspaces, including different entity types.

## Can my accountant or employee upload documents?

Yes. Organisation owners can invite people and give them limited permissions.

## How will I receive updates?

Updates appear in the client workspace and may also be sent through enabled email, SMS or WhatsApp channels.

## Is my information secure?

Access is limited by organisation and role. Sensitive credentials should never be sent through ordinary messages.
