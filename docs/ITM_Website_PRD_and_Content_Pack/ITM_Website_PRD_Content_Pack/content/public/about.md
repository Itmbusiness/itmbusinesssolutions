# Practical Support for Starting and Running an Organisation

ITM Business Solutions helps founders and established organisations navigate formation, compliance, supplier onboarding, licensing and professional business-document services.

## Our approach

- Understand the outcome before recommending a service.
- Explain requirements and limitations in plain language.
- Send a written quotation before requesting payment.
- Keep professional and statutory charges separate.
- Record progress and client actions clearly.
- Maintain support after registration through reminders and recurring services.

## What we do not promise

We do not guarantee authority approval, tender awards or processing dates controlled by third parties.
