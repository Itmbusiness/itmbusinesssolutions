# Terms of Service

Production legal review is required before publication.

The final terms must cover quotations and validity, professional versus statutory fees, payment timing, refunds, client accuracy obligations, authorisations, third-party authority decisions, processing estimates, communication, intellectual property, document storage, account permissions, suspension, termination, complaints and governing law.

Quotation acceptance must identify the exact version and terms accepted.
