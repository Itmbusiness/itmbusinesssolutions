# Product Scope and Boundaries

## Product statement

The platform combines a public SEO website, quotation-led sales workflow and shared service-delivery workspace. It is not a government portal, law firm, accounting package or guarantee of authority approval.

## Core domains

- Discovery and education
- Service recommendations
- Lead and quotation management
- Multi-organisation client workspaces
- Application and document workflow
- Payments after approval
- Compliance dates and reminders
- Article and regulated-content publishing
- Operational reporting

## Boundary rules

- Advice that changes a legal or tax decision requires authorised human review.
- External authority status is labelled “reported” or “confirmed” with source and timestamp.
- The platform never fabricates official reference numbers, certificates or submission states.
- Clients retain responsibility for the completeness and accuracy of source information.
- Consultants record assumptions and obtain approval before critical submissions.
