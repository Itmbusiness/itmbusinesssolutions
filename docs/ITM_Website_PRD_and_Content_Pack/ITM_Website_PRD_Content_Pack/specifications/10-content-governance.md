# Content Governance

## Content classes

### Regulated and high-volatility

Tax, company re-registration, PRAZ, NSSA, PVO, municipal and transport licensing. Review at least monthly and immediately after a verified official change.

### Regulated and moderate-volatility

Company structures, trusts and associations. Review quarterly and after legal or procedural change.

### Commercial and low-volatility

Branding, general process and company-profile content. Review every six months.

## Required metadata

- Author
- Reviewer
- Reviewed on
- Next review date
- Primary official source
- Material assumptions
- Change summary
- Publication status

## Stale-content response

- Notify owner before review date.
- Flag overdue content in the consultant dashboard.
- Suspend price, deadline or eligibility claims first.
- Display a neutral verification notice when current accuracy cannot be confirmed.
- Preserve the previous version and change history.

## Prohibited claims

- Guaranteed authority approval
- Guaranteed tender award
- Unverified “official agent” or regulator endorsement
- Unsupported processing times
- Hidden statutory fees
- Blanket asset-protection or tax-outcome promises
