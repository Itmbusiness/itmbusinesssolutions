# Functional Requirements Catalogue

This file complements the master PRD and provides implementation grouping.

## Public experience

- Responsive page rendering and global search.
- Goal-based service finder.
- Service, category, package and article browsing.
- Requirement-checklist display and download.
- Service-aware quotation and WhatsApp CTAs.
- Guest tracking with limited disclosure.

## Commercial operations

- Lead deduplication and assignment.
- Qualification notes and outcome.
- Versioned quotations with line-item types.
- Approval, rejection, expiry and revision.
- Payment request only after approval.
- Manual and integrated payment confirmation.

## Delivery operations

- Configurable service workflows.
- Requirement and document management.
- Tasks, due dates, assignees and reviewers.
- Client-visible and internal status communication.
- Completion evidence and document handover.

## Client workspace

- Multi-organisation switching.
- Team invitations and permissions.
- Quotes, payments, applications and files.
- Compliance dates and reminders.
- Export and support requests.

## Content operations

- Drafting, review, scheduling and archiving.
- SEO metadata and structured-data fields.
- Review dates and stale-content alerts.
- Redirects and internal-link reporting.
