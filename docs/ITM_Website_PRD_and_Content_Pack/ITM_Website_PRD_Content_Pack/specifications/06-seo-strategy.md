# SEO and Search Strategy

## Search-intent layers

- Homepage: brand and broad corporate-services intent.
- Category pages: broad commercial topics.
- Service pages: high-intent transactional searches.
- Articles: questions, comparisons, requirements and mistake-prevention searches.
- Requirement pages: task-oriented checklist searches.

## On-page standard

Every indexable page requires a unique title, meta description, H1, introduction, useful body content, internal links, visible CTA, canonical URL and updated date where appropriate.

## Internal-link model

Article → relevant service → category → quotation CTA.  
Service → requirements → related services or package → quotation CTA.  
Category → every child service plus relevant articles.

## Structured data

Use Organisation, Service, BreadcrumbList, Article and WebSite structured data only when it matches visible content. FAQ markup must mirror visible questions and answers.

## Content-quality protections

- No copied authority or competitor content.
- No keyword-stuffed city pages.
- No guarantees of approval or processing time.
- No undated regulatory claims.
- No fabricated reviews, author credentials or completion statistics.
- Every regulated page has source and review metadata.

## Technical controls

Separate XML sitemaps, canonical enforcement, redirect management, robots controls, social metadata, descriptive image alternatives, pagination rules, broken-link reporting and staging no-index protection.
